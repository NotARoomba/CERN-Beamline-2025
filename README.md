# Probing Graphene's Anomalous Quantum Hall Effect via Relativistic Electron Beam Scattering

This repository documents an experiment proposal for studying the Quantum Hall Effect in graphene under controlled magnetic fields and relativistic beam conditions. The experiment was submitted for Beamline For Schools 2025 and investigates Landau level spacing, charge symmetry, and Dirac fermion dynamics. 

---

## Key Objectives
- **Landau Level Resolution**: Quantify energy gaps between Landau levels (LLs) under varying magnetic fields.
- **Charge Symmetry Testing**: Compare electron (\(e^-\)) and positron (\(e^+\)) responses to Berry phase effects.
- **Dirac Fermion Dynamics**: Explore momentum-dependent velocity scaling (\(v \propto p\)) and non-equilibrium carrier density.
